House and Room: drag or arrow keys to circle, scroll or + − to zoom, right-drag to pan
Room: G near walls cut low or glass
Walking: WASD move, drag or ←→ to turn, Shift to hurry
Flying: ↑↓ up and down, free of the floor until you come back down onto one
Double-click to lock the mouse for looking, Esc to free it
C next place, [ ] one level down or up
